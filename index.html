1. Follow the evidence, one word at a time

The starting point is the temperature-adjusted class prior. Each retained token adds its log-likelihood ratio. This is a partial-message score, not a separate spam verdict for each word. Move the slider to inspect the running probability.

Temperature adjusts probabilities; displayed log odds and word contributions remain raw Bayes values. Word order changes the intermediate path, but not the final score. Repeated words contribute repeatedly.

2. The Bayes calculation

score(C) = ln P(C) + Σ count(w, message) × ln P(w | C)

Natural logarithms turn products into sums. Subtracting the maximum score before exponentiating prevents numerical underflow.

3. Word-level evidence

P(w | C) = (training count(w, C) + 1) / (total words(C) + |V|)
Contribution = message count(w) × ln[P(w | spam) / P(w | ham)]

Positive values favour spam, negative values favour ham. Common, unseen and rare tokens are ignored. Their probabilities display as a dash and their contribution is zero.
